Work With Me

Historical consulting offer. I no longer offer the Operations Deep Dive, monthly retainers, or other consulting services.

For current professional information, see Fletcher Lite.

What the Consulting Covered

When a company grows fast, things break. Communication slips, work gets repeated, people step on each other, and the founder ends up carrying everything. My consulting helped teams get organized and work more consistently.

  • Create clear workflows that people can follow
  • Write the documentation teams actually use
  • Help teams understand who owns what
  • Reduce confusion between product, dev, support and ops
  • Organize work so it moves at a steady pace
  • Take tasks off the founder's plate

The Operations Deep Dive

The Deep Dive was the starting engagement. I reviewed how a company was running, traced the causes of problems, and recommended fixes.

What the Deep Dive Included

  • A review of your workflows and day to day processes
  • A look at how teams hand work off to each other
  • A check of your tools and how they’re being used
  • A simple explanation of what slows your team down
  • A clear list of fixes to make the company run better
  • A 90 day plan you can follow right away

Timeline

10 days from start to finish

Historical Pricing

The Deep Dive was priced at $1,500 USD. Ongoing consulting was $4,500 USD per month.

How I Worked

  • I made sure people understand what they need to do
  • I helped the founder focus on the important work
  • I addressed issues at the source so they wouldn’t keep coming back
