What This Means in Practice

What I’m Like in a Corporate Environment

In a corporate environment, I often become the person people bring complicated problems to. I stay calm when priorities change. I can work independently while keeping the broader goal in view.

I ask questions early. I document decisions and create structure when ownership or information is unclear. When technical and non-technical teams need to work together, I help them understand each other and decide what happens next.

As a leader, I’m direct and supportive. I set clear expectations, share context, and listen carefully. I prefer helping people work through problems over simply handing them instructions.

I care about the quality of the outcome and the experience of the people producing it. A good system protects both.
