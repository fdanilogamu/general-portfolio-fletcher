What you get when you dig into this site

This site is part portfolio, part laboratory, and part field notebook. I keep what I notice, learn, make, experience, and wonder about. Sometimes pieces connect in a way that matters; I give that connection enough form to meet reality and see what happens.

Resident Inventor is the professional identity I use for that tendency. The medium follows the idea, so the results don’t always resemble each other.

Professional work and playful work coexist here because they keep informing each other. Some projects are finished, some are active, and others are snapshots of my thinking in motion.

For the quick version, start with the TL;DR. Otherwise, follow whichever title makes you curious: the pages are more connected than they first appear.
