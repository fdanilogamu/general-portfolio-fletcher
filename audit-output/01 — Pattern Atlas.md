# Pattern Atlas

The clearest recurring problem is **compensation that disappears into a successful outcome**. A meeting can keep an unclear decision process functioning. A person can remember the context a task form forgot. An interviewer can improvise around a weak packet. The work repeatedly moves some of that compensation into an inspectable artifact. It also exposes a limit. Sometimes the compensating person is supplying judgment that the artifact should preserve or invite, rather than absorb.

## Evidence boundary

Generated Docusaurus output, dependencies and migration fixtures were treated as copies or infrastructure unless they documented a specific change. The site is a selective archive. [Source homepage](../index.html), [navigation](../site-navigation.html)

**Direct** means the local artifact encodes the behavior or is itself the creative work. **Reported** means a case study or retrospective describes a design, implementation or event. **Concept** means the record describes an idea or intended form. Direct evidence supports claims about what was specified or built in this repository. Adoption, outcomes and transfer remain separate questions.

### What the published writing itself shows

The Sales Orders guide distinguishes mandatory fields from the subset usually needed for a basic order. Choosing an account supplies shipping terms, which users can override, and alters which related fields are available. The guide teaches an interaction where the system carries context and the operator retains discretion. Customer Accounts describes a route that lets the sales team go directly to account work without needing to understand the full company model or access sensitive company data. That is an older, concrete instance of the same concern Lemonless later expresses in creating a child item from a known parent. The two PDFs also carry other names in their ownership or update metadata. They show what the samples encode, with indeterminate individual authorship of specific passages. The air-gap and failover PDFs did not yield extractable text in this review. Those two are inventoried at the exhibit-description level. ## Seven recurring patterns

| Lens | Observation and interpretation | Tension or exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Problem recognition** | The Mitigation Trap, context re-entry in Lemonless and the Lemonade guide's failed form all expose work performed to compensate for a system. A promising diagnostic question is what a successful result cost the person who made it look routine. | PRISON PLANET treats some upkeep as intrinsic, and The Way They See It investigates variation without presupposing dysfunction. [OEI and Lemonade histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [case study](../0-software-and-ai/lemonless-tms-case-study.html), [satire](../0-music-and-mischief/prison-planet.html) |
| **Solution architecture** | Find the smallest carrier that preserves the causal behavior. Keep support geometry through a chair redesign, choices through a medium change, or a stance across assistant platforms. | The Institute grew a new governing layer when portability depended on preserving practitioner competence. Smaller artifacts sometimes require larger institutions. [Anchorpoint, CYOA and OEI loop 6](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [Porpoise specs](../porpoise/stances/) |
| **Human behavior** | Context can be lost in seconds or reactivated years later. Lemonade narrows choices for an immediate state, while the AI collaboration account brings old material back to trigger recognition. | The Drafter deliberately adds effort between stages. Inglés Rebelde offers exposition alongside curiosity. Motivation and attention seem contingent on the task and moment.
