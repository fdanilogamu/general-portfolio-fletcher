# Invention Hypotheses

The order weighs conceptual distance from the documented work first, source strength second and the ability of a small experiment to distinguish explanations third. Adjacent positions are judgment calls. Confidence describes the evidence for the opportunity and the transfer from source to proposed mechanism. All mechanisms and experiments below are conjectures. Novelty is relative to the audited record.

## 1. Two-sided closure

- **Problem** — A helper can finish exactly what was requested while the reason for requesting help persists. Closing or reopening a shared task conceals which party believes which thing happened.
- **Evidence trail** — Lemonless automatically resolves linked support on completion. The Lemonade reference models different cognitive jobs for the two sides. Rabbit Hole distinguishes reservation from arrival. [Support rules](../0-software-and-ai/lemonless-tms-case-study.html), [Lemonade history](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [session behavior](../RABBIT-HOLE.md)
- **Emergent insight** — Fulfilment and experienced effect can be independently true or false. They need different witnesses.
- **Proposed mechanism** — A small handoff protocol lets the actor record delivery and the recipient optionally record relief, a remaining need or uncertainty. A mismatch stays legible without automatically assigning fresh work or demanding agreement.
- **Novelty** — The unit is a pair of independently owned claims. The existing linked-task mechanism treats one state change as evidence for both.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Use two paper slips per handoff for six to ten routine help requests. Compare with similar one-status handoffs. Count cases where slips surface a useful discrepancy earlier and check who spent time recording it.
- **Uncertainties** — The recipient could experience the second signal as a new obligation. Disagreement must lead somewhere useful often enough to justify the added step.
- **Confidence** — High in the design conflict. Moderate in a low-burden protocol that works across contexts.

## 2. Observer field-of-view trial

- **Problem** — Teams interpreting ambiguous incidents can mistake different access to evidence for factual disagreement or unequal competence.
- **Evidence trail** — The Way They See It holds a stimulus constant across trained observers and recognizes camera framing. OEI assigns different status to source claims and identifies the customer as a downstream observer. ECH elicits evidence through designed scenarios. [Media concept, OEI loops 3–4 and ECH loop 1](../_data/resident_inventor.json)
- **Emergent insight** — Evidence availability and directed attention can be varied independently. Agreement on conclusions is interpretable only once both are understood.
- **Proposed mechanism** — Run an observation exercise with private first passes, a shared inventory of available cues and a second pass after observers exchange what they noticed. Track omissions, interpretations of a shared observation, and genuine factual contradictions separately.
- **Novelty** — The output is a map of complementary sensing and its limits. The medium becomes an instrument that deliberately varies cue availability and trains teams to characterize their disagreements.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Give two groups one short staged service scene, one from a fixed camera and one with cropped context. Collect notices and interpretations, exchange one observation per person, then re-evaluate. Success is a demonstrable movement between types of disagreement, with visible counterexamples.
- **Uncertainties** — The scene needs enough ambiguity to reveal differences. Participants may converge socially after exchanging observations.
- **Confidence** — Strong conceptual support. The underlying media format and empirical transfer are unverified.

## 3. Maintenance interruption ledger

- **Problem** — A maintenance activity can be necessary and well executed while repeatedly disrupting the work it sustains.
- **Evidence trail** — PRISON PLANET includes resumption as a dependency in a bodily detour. Lemonless treats completed work as visible momentum. OEI distinguishes compensation from repair. [Known issues and demands](../0-music-and-mischief/prison-planet.html), [Momentum](../0-software-and-ai/lemonless-tms-case-study.html), [Mitigation Trap](../_data/resident_inventor.json)
- **Emergent insight** — Recurrence, recurrence avoidability and resumption cost are separate. A model of maintenance can preserve renewal while reducing disruption.
- **Proposed mechanism** — Record what must recur, the risk if the service lapses, and what ongoing activity must be reconstructed afterwards. Use that record to identify a minimal resumption cue or staging point before a detour.
- **Novelty** — The state being maintained is continuity across repeated interruptions. A recurring task plus completion credit tracks a different outcome.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Alternate ordinary interruptions with a scrap of paper recording the next intended action before predictable routine detours. Compare restart latency and errors, including the time spent leaving the cue. Check whether the ledger's prior classification helps identify which detours merit the cue.
- **Uncertainties** — Logging can create more disruption than it removes. Some detours are unpredictable, and apparent improvement may come from any written reminder.
- **Confidence** — Strong fit with the source tension. Moderate for usefulness over a generic resumption cue.

## 4. Blind handoff for tacit authority

- **Problem** — A method may appear transferable until the originator is absent and a practitioner must depart from precedent or refuse to conclude.
- **Evidence trail** — OEI identified founder dependency and describes assessment environments with auditable reasoning. Anchorpoint and CYOA identify what should survive a medium shift. Porpoise relies on a host to enact its specification. [Founder dependency, Anchorpoint and CYOA histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [assessment entry](../show-and-tell/updates.js), [stance files](../porpoise/stances/)
- **Emergent insight** — Transfer can fail because a cue was missing, because permission to act was missing, or because neither party can reconstruct the rationale after the fact.
- **Proposed mechanism** — A transfer test applicable to methods outside OEI. Provide equivalent ambiguous cases and vary only access to examples, original author decisions or explicit permission to halt. Score defensible divergence and appropriate abstention alongside reproducibility. Use the failures to localize which type of support traveled poorly.
- **Novelty** — Tests the completeness and authority boundaries of the teaching artifact, holding practitioner skill as constant as feasible. OEI's described assessment focuses on practitioner competence.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Have two competent peers try the same unfamiliar, low-stakes method across two counterbalanced paper cases. Provide an explicit abstention rule on one case per person and a boundary example on the other. Compare their reasoning to a prewritten set of defensible outcomes.
- **Uncertainties** — The author may already test this in the external certification work. Results need a second method and more than one trained practitioner to generalize.
- **Confidence** — High in founder dependency as a reported design problem. Moderate in novelty relative to the unseen assessment.

## 5. Counterfactual retrieval drill

- **Problem** — A searchable archive can consistently fail to reintroduce useful material when people frame a new problem differently from its original filing category.
- **Evidence trail** — The ECH artifact reportedly survived dormancy and was reactivated during deliberate archaeology. Rabbit Hole separates approved exposure, selection and arrival. The job-search exhibit compresses career events for later access. [ECH loop 1](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [Rabbit Hole registry and session behavior](../RABBIT-HOLE.md), [timeline data](../0-experience/job-search/job-search-timeline.html)
- **Emergent insight** — Failure to encounter can be measured with the archive held fixed and the retrieval cue varied.
- **Proposed mechanism** — Select known relevant and decoy records in advance. Give participants an unfamiliar problem and compare keyword search, curator-selected adjacencies and constrained serendipity. Score whether recovered material changes a plausible next decision, alongside distraction and false analogies.
- **Novelty** — The output is an estimate of which useful records a retrieval method leaves dormant. A random tour changes exposure without any task-specific measure of what useful material remained unseen.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Use printed abstracts of twelve older items and three short problem briefs. Rotate retrieval methods across readers. Have a blind reviewer evaluate changed decisions against evidence in the records.
- **Uncertainties** — Preselected relevance may privilege ideas already legible to the organizer. Novel discoveries outside the answer key need explicit review.
- **Confidence** — High in the distinction between retention and exposure. Moderate in a reproducible usefulness measure.

## 6. Burden displacement rehearsal

- **Problem** — An improvement can lower visible workload for the person who owns a metric while moving coordination or uncertainty onto someone else.
- **Evidence trail** — The Mitigation Trap describes compensation, the Lemonade reference offers paired views, and Coltejer reports tracing waste to upstream reprocessing. [OEI and Lemonade histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [Coltejer whitening project](../0-experience/jobs-prior-to-2020/coltejer.md)
- **Emergent insight** — Removing a step from one perspective can remove work, transfer work or create a new dependency. A one-role before-and-after test confounds all three.
- **Proposed mechanism** — Rehearse a proposed change twice with different participants holding each role. Record where waiting, re-entry, clarification and discretionary judgment move. A third observer represents a downstream recipient and checks the resulting service.
- **Novelty** — Compares distribution of work before a change is adopted. The nearby OEI method investigates operational problems, and the paired reference describes a problem from two positions.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Run a synthetic three-person scheduling workflow with and without approval. Compare elapsed time, clarifications and downstream corrections by role. Swap actors and see whether the displacement travels with the role.
- **Uncertainties** — Real burdens may appear only at scale. The external OEI practice could already have a closely related intervention simulation.
- **Confidence** — Strong evidence for the concern. Moderate for conceptual distance given the missing methodology corpus.

## 7. A ledger of useful wrong turns

- **Problem** — Finished artifacts conceal the mistaken proposals that made their decisive distinctions visible, and retrospectives make uneven discovery paths look orderly.
- **Evidence trail** — The AI collaboration exhibit treats rejected candidates as productive. ECH preserves an explicit gap in its intermediate record. The Lab records what prompts supplied and what the creator supplied. [Cheap collisions and real runs](../0-software-and-ai/how-i-collaborate-with-ai.html), [ECH and Lab histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json)
- **Emergent insight** — The reusable unit might be a boundary discovered by rejecting a proposal, with the rejected proposal serving as its contrast case.
- **Proposed mechanism** — Capture a tiny pair at the moment of revision. Record the tempting rejected move and a testable distinction it exposed. Revisit pairs under changed conditions and flag distinctions that stop holding.
- **Novelty** — The record is indexed by reversible propositions produced by rejection. General session archiving and histories preserve much more material but do not foreground this unit or test whether its boundary travels.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Extract six pairs from old traces. Give peers matched new cases and either the pairs or equally short accounts of final outcomes. Check whether the pairs help identify boundaries and exceptions without inviting stale rules.
- **Uncertainties** — Condensation can rewrite hindsight into the record. The same brief note could perform as well without a paired structure.
- **Confidence** — Strong evidence for productive rejections as a method. Moderate novelty because the exhibit already preserves compressed working traces.

## 8. Dream-source separation cards

- **Problem** — An evocative visualization can become part of what a person later reports remembering, making a recurring motif difficult to attribute.
- **Evidence trail** — Dream Machine would put renderings and comparisons into one collection. The Archivist preserves reasoning and context, and the OEI history distinguishes claims from corroboration. [Dream Machine and OEI loop 3](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [Archivist method](../porpoise/stances/archivist.yaml)
- **Emergent insight** — A rendered scene is both an output and a possible intervention on the next observation.
- **Proposed mechanism** — Keep a pre-visualization account, a record of rendering additions and a later recall as distinct layers. Compare motifs by source, with an explicit category for unresolved origin.
- **Novelty** — The instrument measures how representation may influence recall. A richer dream journal or a rendering feature would serve a different purpose.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — With consent, use invented brief scene accounts and hand-drawn variants, some subtly introducing an object. After a delay compare later recall on illustrated and unillustrated examples, tracking foreign elements and attribution separately.
- **Uncertainties** — An invented scene lacks many properties of dream recall. A difference could reflect rehearsal or stimulus salience. No repository evidence establishes that this contamination occurs here.
- **Confidence** — Low for the dream-specific effect. Moderate for the need to distinguish source layers if comparison follows visualization.

## 9. A removable pause trial

- **Problem** — Workflows remove pauses for speed without measuring whether the pause supplied correction, learning or authorization.
- **Evidence trail** — The Drafter gates Read, Resist and Redraft. EAFIT reports rehearsal intended to push engagement. Inglés Rebelde offers different routes into material. [Drafter workflow and iteration](../porpoise/stances/drafter.yaml), [EAFIT rehearsal](../0-experience/jobs-prior-to-2020/eafit.md), [learning paths](../0-teaching-and-learning/ingles-rebelde/inglesrebelde.html)
- **Emergent insight** — A pause can have multiple jobs. A wait, an elicited decision and a review with alternatives deserve separate treatment.
- **Proposed mechanism** — Use interchangeable gates in a human workflow. Compare elapsed waiting, active judgment and continuous processing while preserving material and decision rights. Keep the smallest interaction that preserves the observed benefit.
- **Novelty** — A method for testing the value of a gate across workflows. Another prompting stance or another staged revision template would remain close to the Drafter.
- **Smallest useful experiment** — Counterbalance three short editing exercises with continuous editing, a timed pause and an explicit decision between two revision rationales. Compare factual errors, explanation quality and time against a prewritten rubric.
- **Uncertainties** — An active gate also adds attention and instruction. An equal-time control would be necessary if the first trial shows a benefit.
- **Confidence** — Strong source basis for gate design. Lower conceptual novelty because staged refinement and rehearsal are already present.

## Adversarial review

- **Technology can create false recurrence.** Jekyll gives the portfolio structured registries, Markdown and stable pages. Transaction-like bookkeeping is an ordinary response to browser navigation and can explain Rabbit Hole without invoking a philosophy of trust. The cross-domain claim was narrowed to the observable gap between action, confirmation and accounting. Evidence from physical support, theater-like observation, teaching and factory work gives the broader comparisons a separate footing. [History architecture](../RESIDENT-INVENTOR.md), [Rabbit Hole implementation notes](../RABBIT-HOLE.md), [Coltejer](../0-experience/jobs-prior-to-2020/coltejer.md)
- **The corpus favors stories that crystallized.** The Lab example shows one generated constraint set and one surviving resolution. The job-search record presents selected phases. The histories use a common narrative model, which can make diverse trajectories appear more uniform. The Atlas credits coded behaviors, preserved uncertainties and concrete reversals more heavily than repeated labels. [Lab and ECH histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [job-search timeline data](../0-experience/job-search/job-search-timeline.html)
- **Repackaging check.** A recurring-task tracker, a random archive browser, a universal AI stance controller and an interviewer generator were screened out. The transferable authority trial and burden rehearsal remain vulnerable to overlap with external OEI materials and rank below the three strongest directions. The wrong-turn ledger is restricted to testable boundaries extracted from rejection, since a searchable history already exists as a stated method. [AI collaboration, History returns](../0-software-and-ai/how-i-collaborate-with-ai.html), [ECH loop 2](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [OEI practitioner assessment record](../show-and-tell/updates.js)
- **Counterexamples constrain the model.** Rabbit Hole delegates a choice to chance. The Drafter imposes deliberate friction. ECH retains a human judge but dates the role and categories to organizational needs. The playlists remain an instance of curation for taste and use, with little evidence of diagnostic ambition. These exceptions limit claims about control, convenience and a single motivating philosophy. [Rabbit Hole](../RABBIT-HOLE.md), [Drafter interaction rules](../porpoise/stances/drafter.yaml), [ECH history](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [playlist index](../0-music-and-mischief/spotify/spotify.md)
- **Attractive and weak.** The visual influence hypothesis remains low confidence. The scope was reduced from building a richer Dream Machine to a test of attribution after viewing. Likewise no claim is made that three randomized prompts reliably produce inventions. That would require discarded runs and a meaningful comparison. [Dream Machine and Inventor’s Lab histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json)
- **Evidence with the most power to reorder the list.** Inspect actual Lemonless support sessions for independent beneficiary status, OEI assessment cases for transfer tests, ECH packets for controls on observer disagreement, and untouched early project materials for examples that failed to fit the eventual narrative. Absent these materials, the ranking favors experiments that can produce informative negative results.

## Three directions worth investigating

1. **Two-sided closure** exposes a relationship that the existing work, reference and navigation models each represent differently. A brief trial could reveal whether allowing distinct claims improves coordination or simply creates another job for the person requesting help.
2. **Observer field-of-view trial** puts a creative format and a diagnostic method into productive conflict. The result could change how you decide which disagreements require resolution and which reveal that the team sees from several useful positions.
3. **Maintenance interruption ledger** lets a satire challenge a design objective. It asks whether acknowledgment of recurrence and ease of returning to interrupted activity explain more than completion counts. Its core experiment can fail cheaply and still reveal which interruptions require a different model.

**What in your work gets treated as finished because only the person who performed it gets to say whether it worked?**
