# Unexpected Connections

The strongest links here connect projects that make different decisions about the same mechanism. A household support tool and a random portfolio tour each define when a transaction is over. A prompt library and an invention history each define when a machine should stop and leave room for judgment. Those differences are more informative than a shared vocabulary.

| Relationship | What travels between the projects | What the comparison puts in question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rabbit Hole ↔ LemonlessTMS ↔ Porpoise distribution | The difference between a request, an event and its accounting | Who gets to certify completion? |
| The Way They See It ↔ OEI ↔ ECH | The same material can produce different observations and judgments | Where does the observer become part of the instrument? |
| PRISON PLANET ↔ LemonlessTMS ↔ OEI | Upkeep and the burden that returns after visible work ends | When is durable completion the wrong objective? |
| Drafter ↔ EAFIT rehearsal ↔ Inventor’s Lab | Added effort can produce a useful second look | Which inconvenience is doing real work? |
| Anchorpoint ↔ CYOA ↔ Porpoise ↔ OEI Institute | Preserve an operation while replacing what carries it | What survives when the inventor is the medium being replaced? |
| ECH archaeology ↔ Rabbit Hole ↔ job-search archive | What is retained, eligible for encounter and actually revisited | How would you notice a failure of rediscovery? |
| Dream Machine ↔ Coltejer ↔ Lemonade Economy | Where a signal appears can differ from where it originated | Can the artifact keep those origins distinguishable? |

## 1. A completed event can leave its purpose outstanding

**Observation.** The Rabbit Hole reserves a selection then commits it on arrival. Porpoise can deliver without recording a count. Lemonless reportedly closes the originating request when the linked helper work is completed. The tour waits for evidence of arrival. The work system accepts a related actor's state change. The API keeps measurement outside the critical path. [Rabbit Hole session behavior](../RABBIT-HOLE.md), [Porpoise download handler](../porpoise-api/lib/handlers.js), [Lemonless support rules](../0-software-and-ai/lemonless-tms-case-study.html)

**Interpretation.** These are three answers to the same question about commitment, arrived at through navigation, distribution and human help. The reusable mechanism is to distinguish the event performed, the effect experienced and the record of either. The analogy ends where human experience enters. Browser arrival can be checked directly, while relief depends on the recipient and sometimes arrives later.

**Question.** Which systems would work better if two parties could disagree about whether something finished, without making either responsible for keeping the ticket open?

## 2. Different observers may be instruments with different sensing ranges

**Observation.** The Way They See It holds the scene constant to expose trained salience and recognizes that the camera can cue attention. OEI distinguishes staff reports, founder claims and operational traces, while treating the customer as a downstream sensor. ECH changes the questions used to elicit evidence according to the conditions of the role. [The Way They See It, OEI loops 3–4 and ECH loop 1](../_data/resident_inventor.json)

**Interpretation.** This is a recurring principle with an unresolved contradiction. The media format prizes differences in what gets noticed. The institutional method must sometimes compare judgments across practitioners. Disagreement can come from different fields of view, inaccessible evidence or incompatible claims about the same fact. Combining those in a single agreement score would discard the distinction the film format is designed to reveal.

**Question.** Could an investigation first map what each observer was positioned to notice, then evaluate where their accounts actually conflict?

## 3. Some work can be completed only for the current cycle

**Observation.** PRISON PLANET makes permanent completion a comically impossible requirement and includes resuming the interrupted task in a bodily detour's dependency tree. Lemonless rewards completed work through Momentum and explicitly relates completion to history. OEI's Mitigation Trap asks whether repeated compensatory activity is keeping structural defects invisible. [PRISON PLANET, Known issues](../0-music-and-mischief/prison-planet.html), [Lemonless Momentum and history](../0-software-and-ai/lemonless-tms-case-study.html), [OEI loop 2](../_data/resident_inventor.json)

**Interpretation.** The satire is a counterexample to treating all recurrence as evidence of bad design. Maintain the distinction between necessary renewal, avoidable rework and unpaid effort to re-enter the activity that maintenance interrupted. This is a recurring concern with a new distinction, supported by the text and still untested in practice.

**Question.** If the target were ease of resuming rather than speed of completing, which maintenance systems would appear successful for the first time?

## 4. Friction can protect an act of judgment

**Observation.** The Drafter stops between passes and postpones revision until after reading and evaluation. EAFIT's rehearsal project describes intentionally moving learners beyond comfortable performance. The Lab uses unrelated inputs to make the inventor resolve an underdetermined situation. Lemonade's first guide, by contrast, imposed a long reading sequence on someone who might need immediate help. [Drafter workflow and interaction rules](../porpoise/stances/drafter.yaml), [EAFIT rehearsal](../0-experience/jobs-prior-to-2020/eafit.md), [Lab and Lemonade histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json)

**Interpretation.** Chosen effort can reveal a distinction, exercise a skill or demand a decision. The issue is who bears the effort and what it produces. Three sources suggest a conditional principle. The pedagogical result and the Lab's reliability remain author-reported.

**Question.** What evidence would justify keeping an interruption in a process if everyone using it asks to make the process faster?

## 5. A transplant reveals what the old medium was doing for free

**Observation.** Anchorpoint shifts the support geometry from a whole chair to an attachment. CYOA shifts exploration from simulated movement to navigation through a book. Porpoise specifies attention in a portable file that relies on a host assistant to enact it. OEI found that transferring a methodology beyond its founder required adding institutional capabilities. [Anchorpoint, CYOA and OEI loops 5–6](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [Porpoise specifications](../porpoise/stances/)

**Interpretation.** Hardware, narrative and governance make a common problem visible. A change of carrier separates explicit rules from affordances the old carrier supplied implicitly. A chair contributes stability. A screen tracks state. An inventor supplies tacit judgments about ambiguous cases. This is a reusable test strategy. The apparent simplicity of a new carrier says little about how much work it displaces.

**Question.** If a method can be reproduced on standard cases but fails only when someone must exercise permission to say “we cannot conclude,” what exactly transferred?

## 6. A well-preserved record can still be functionally lost

**Observation.** The ECH history describes the original hiring artifact lying dormant and then being quickly found during deliberate archaeology. Its intermediate evolution remains missing. Rabbit Hole defines which accessible exhibits are eligible for a chance encounter and which encounters count. The job-search timeline makes selected changes newly retrievable in phases. [ECH loop 1](../_data/resident_inventor.json), [Rabbit Hole registry and session rules](../RABBIT-HOLE.md), [job-search timeline data](../0-experience/job-search/job-search-timeline.html)

**Interpretation.** Searchability, eligibility and actual re-encounter are distinct conditions. The portfolio accidentally supplies a formal model of what a curated archive fails to surface. The archive can have complete objects and a biased encounter distribution simultaneously. This is a reusable mechanism with a speculative organizational application.

**Question.** Can you measure the work an archive quietly makes impossible by ensuring a person will almost never encounter a relevant record outside its original category?

## 7. The visible record can inherit causes from elsewhere

**Observation.** Coltejer's whitening account traces waste to upstream reprocessing. The Lemonade history locates some friction in a mismatch between a person and an environment. Dream Machine adds comparison and recurrence to rendering, which introduces a question about whether an element came from recall or visualization. [Coltejer whitening project](../0-experience/jobs-prior-to-2020/coltejer.md), [Lemonade and Dream Machine histories](../_data/resident_inventor.json)

**Interpretation.** The shared move is to examine the production of the signal before attributing it. The dream connection is weaker. It raises a plausible measurement problem, while the history specifies no source-separation procedure and supplies no evidence of visualization altering later recall.

**Question.** How would a dream archive detect a pattern it had itself helped create?

## Negative space

| Opening | An extension | A new direction and its missing ingredient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fulfilment without experienced relief** | Add a reopen button to Lemonless. | Treat actor completion, beneficiary effect and coordination cost as independently assertable facts in a protocol usable even without a task system. Its missing ingredient is a low-burden way to hold disagreement open. |
| **Limits of agreement** | Add another evidence field or interviewer rubric. | Run matched observations with reciprocal visibility maps, then compare observations and interpretations separately. Its missing ingredient is a neutral baseline for who could see what. |
| **Maintenance without finish lines** | Add recurrence and reminders. | Represent service continuity and interruption debt as primary states. Its missing ingredient is a way to tell unavoidable renewal from interventions that merely displace work. |
| **Half-lives of recoverable ideas** | Add more destinations to the tour or backlinks to the archive. | Make the discoverability of dormant assumptions or fragments experimentally testable using delayed, changed-context retrieval. Its missing ingredient is a comparison that holds the archive constant while varying encounter cues. |
| **Tacit authority in transfer** | Produce more training material or an additional practitioner case. | Compare what breaks when the founder is absent, and attribute failure to missing cues, permission or examples. Its missing ingredient is a blind handoff across contexts where the evaluator can observe legitimate refusal to conclude. |
| **Upstream burden transfer** | Add a new OEI dimension or score. | Compare a proposed change across whose work falls, whose rises and whose competence the revised system requires. Its missing ingredient is matched observation across positions, including people outside the immediate workflow. |

The archive here already preserves several productive unresolved states, including project histories whose missing stages stay visible and a tour whose failed navigation leaves the destination eligible. The next question is whether **keeping something unresolved can itself be designed as a transfer of agency**. A system could preserve the right of a future participant to reinterpret an event without requiring anyone to constantly manage that open state.
