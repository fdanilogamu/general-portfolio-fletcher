Follow a question

What can an idea keep when it changes form?

A chair, a game and a guide each change form to make their original purpose easier to reach.

  • Anchorpoint / LeanMate carries a posture-support idea from a whole chair to an attachment.
  • CYOA Video Game Graphic Novels asks which choices and puzzles can survive the move from screen to page.
  • The Lemonade Economy history traces a long guide’s shift into a reference people can enter at the problem they recognize.

What changes when someone else looks?

Keep the scene, situation or problem the same and see how roles and habits of attention change what comes into view.

  • The Way They See It proposes showing one scene to people trained in different fields.
  • Porpoise’s conversation examples illustrate what different stances notice in the same situation.
  • The Lemonade Economy history shows why the person needing help and the person offering it get different routes into the same material.

What work is hiding in a result that looks routine?

A process can look fine because someone keeps absorbing the extra work. These examples look for that work in different places.

  • Operational Entropy Index history describes how meetings and oversight can hide structural strain.
  • LemonlessTMS case study gives a helper’s work its own assignment and record.
  • Coltejer experience recounts how a whitening project traced waste to rework earlier in production.
