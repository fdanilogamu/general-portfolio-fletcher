Inventions & Frameworks

  • Resident Inventor

    An interactive model built from the histories of nine inventions.

    Choose a tab on that page to explore Dream Machine, Anchorpoint / LeanMate, The Way They See It, CYOA Video Game Graphic Novels, OEI, ECH, Porpoise, The Lemonade Economy, or Inventor’s Lab and its Masked Tuk-Tuk Driver example. These histories include concepts and evolving work, not just released products.

  • Operational Entropy IndexExternal

    I developed OEI to investigate operational strain across five pillars. The OEI Institute governs the methodology and its practice standards, and develops practitioner training, assessment, and authorization pathways.

    On the OEI site: the methodology · the Institute.

  • The Lemonade EconomyExternal

    An evolving reference system with paired perspectives for people with executive dysfunction and the people supporting them.
